Asking price and recreation estimate

The asking reference is the seller's indicative acquisition amount for the present first-party asset. The recreation estimate is a seller-side engineering budget to recreate comparable current work. They are different references. No new price has been calculated for this exhibition; figures are copied from the published 13 October 2026 pricing register.

The documented pricing approach

The retained method anchors asking references primarily to estimated recreation effort, then applies asset-specific choices for maturity, remaining qualification, transfer conditions and buyer productisation. Future software, physical inventions, revenue forecasts and unsupported scientific outcomes are outside the present-scope estimate.

Seller assumptions remain assumptions

The retained role budgets are EUR 18,000 per senior-engineer month, EUR 22,000 per specialist month and EUR 16,000 per validation/provenance month, at an assumed 160 hours per month. Those imply EUR 112.50, EUR 137.50 and EUR 100 per hour. Effort estimates, a 15% coordination/tooling allowance, rounding and asking-price discounts are seller-side analyst assumptions rather than recorded hours or observed transactions.

A disclosed example: MIPP

The published MIPP v0.1 scope uses 2.25 senior-engineer months, 0.50 specialist months and 0.75 validation/provenance months: EUR 63,500 direct budget plus EUR 9,525 allowance, giving EUR 73,025. The adopted recreation reference is EUR 70,000 and the seller asking reference is 30% of that amount: EUR 21,000. This example applies to MIPP's priced current scope; it is not a uniform pricing formula for all assets.

Interpret the references appropriately

A displayed asking price is not a completed sale. A recreation estimate is not a certified cost audit, market value, guaranteed saving or promise of future revenue. No new independent appraisal, attributable income analysis or precedent transaction assessment was conducted for this exhibition. Final price and fitness are agreed for the buyer's specific transaction.
