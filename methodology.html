Role budgets are EUR 18,000 per senior-engineer month, EUR 22,000 per specialist month and EUR 16,000 per validation/provenance month at 160 assumed hours. The implied EUR 112.50, EUR 137.50 and EUR 100 hourly budgets exceed the Knab mean. A disclosed example: MIPP

The priced v0.1 repository scope uses 2.25 senior months, 0.50 specialist months and 0.75 validation/provenance months: EUR 63,500 direct plus EUR 9,525 allowance. The EUR 73,025 budget is rounded to a EUR 70,000 recreation anchor. Its EUR 21,000 ask is a seller-selected 30% of that anchor. Later staged application source is excluded. This is not a uniform formula for the other projects.

Internal scenarios and unestablished valuations

The hub's marketability, monetization and strategic figures share the same recreation anchor. Their weighted blend is a correlated internal sensitivity, not independent triangulation. No comparable-sale valuation, attributable project-income valuation or independent appraisal is established. A seller ask is not a completed sale, and a recreation anchor is not guaranteed buyer savings.
