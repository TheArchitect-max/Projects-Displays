Current source versus priced delivery

The current main and unmerged development candidate are separate from the older cost-estimate scope. New documentation does not establish that all newer work has been priced, delivered or qualified. The 16 October review retains individual amounts, identifies missing or partial budgets, and requires a delivery-specific re-estimate before reliance. Original qualification and price-basis dates are retained.

Presentation and scientific boundaries

Potential buyer profiles and illustrative evaluation uses are positioning suggestions inferred from documented functions. They do not establish customers, revenue, field accuracy, comparative superiority or regulatory approval. Research objectives and proposed integrations remain distinct from delivered functions.

Disclosure boundary

This exhibition contains product identity, use, functions, outputs, status, limitations, seller-side commercial references and high-level rights/transfer scope. It contains no underlying product implementation, internal architecture, algorithms, proprietary parameters, component lists, secrets, private datasets or internal development know-how. Ordinary display styling and enquiry controls belong only to this presentation package.

Reuse and access

Receiving this display grants access to the presentation for review. It does not grant a licence to operate, copy, distribute, disclose or commercialise any underlying product. Separate evidence review and transfer rights are governed by written terms.
