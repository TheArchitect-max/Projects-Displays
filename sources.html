Valuation methods and disclosure

Cost-based evaluation, disclosed assumptions and the distinction between engineering cost and negotiated value.

https://www.wipo.int/web-publications/intellectual-property-valuation-basics-for-technology-transfer-professionals/en/4-the-cost-method.html

Source finding
Cost-based reproduction/replacement is a starting point; cost and negotiated value may differ.
Application in the evaluation
Method selection and the cost-versus-value boundary; no asset amount or discount is supplied by WIPO.

Engineering rate context

https://bieb.knab.nl/ondernemen/wat-verdient-een-software-developer-zzp-bekijk-uurtarief-en-winst

Source finding
Reported 2026 mean software-developer rate: EUR 94/hour excluding VAT; broader survey includes over 20,000 Dutch self-employed people.
Application in the evaluation
External Dutch rate cross-check. The retained EUR 100–137.50/hour role assumptions reflect an analyst-selected specialist budget and exceed this mean; the mean is not claimed to validate them.
