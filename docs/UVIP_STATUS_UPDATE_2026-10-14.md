## Present functions and direction

The main prototype provides playback controls, timestamp bookmarks, private human-authored research notes, per-video drafts and local JSON backup recovery. Its separate offline research workflow supports heuristic project matching, human review, source-linked investigation proposals and read-only evidence checks. It does not automatically change target repositories or prove research claims.

The development candidate adds read-only local-library search, stricter archive/restore integrity, confirmed-write reconciliation, trusted controls and draft/focus recovery across navigation and panel remounts. R28 checks a narrow set of live launcher/editor, video-navigation, Escape/focus/ARIA and popup/settings scenarios; it does not qualify every feature or browser workflow.

Permission-aware transcription, OCR, multimodal analysis, cross-video knowledge, evidence-grounded AI and a standalone workspace or API remain future implementation and qualification objectives. The exploratory AI direction is not represented as a delivered or commercially qualified capability.
