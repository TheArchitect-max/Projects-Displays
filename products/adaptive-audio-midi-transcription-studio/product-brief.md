# Adaptive Audio MIDI Transcription Studio

TA-IP-085 | Reference alias: Aria | Audio & Media Creation

Buyer-facing edition: 16 October 2026

Turn your recordings into editable MIDI tracks, inspect the notes and take individual parts into your music workflow.

## Product overview

An audio-transcription prototype for editable, inspectable music tracks.

## Documented functions

- Import supported recordings
- Separate source parts when configured
- Create approximate note tracks
- Inspect tracks in a piano roll
- Export individual or combined MIDI

## Potential buyers

- Music software vendors
- Production studios
- Creator-tool developers

Buyer profiles are positioning suggestions, not established customers or contracts.

## Documented output types

- Editable note tracks
- Piano-roll views
- Multitrack MIDI files

## Status and limitations

Current main baseline: No semantic release version established.
