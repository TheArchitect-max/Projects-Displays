## Product overview

A standalone local-first chess analysis and training prototype with legally synchronised board history, bounded native research analysers and a separate owner-interface pathway.

## Potential acquirers

- Chess and training-software vendors
- Educational game and practice-platform developers
- Research teams evaluating chess-search methods

## Documented functions

- Maintain legal board and move-history state
- Review positions from either side
- Support local practice and actual-move correction
- Compare bounded native search and history-aware caches
- Review reproducible training and analysis records

## Output types

- Position and move-history records
- Bounded native analysis reports
- Practice and evaluation packages

## Material scope boundaries

- The native v1.28 research analyser and its history-safe cache have local software qualification; independent v1.28 acceptance remains pending. The owner interface retains its separately accepted earlier engine pathway.
- World-class strength, verified Elo, guaranteed wins and strength attributable to an optional third-party engine are not established properties of the proprietary native engine.
- Historical game and derived opening-data capsules retain CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution and share-alike terms. Optional external engines retain their own licences and are outside the proprietary first-party price.
