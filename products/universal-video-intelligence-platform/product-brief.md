# Universal Video Intelligence Platform

TA-IP-101 | Reference alias: Ophelia | Audio & Media Creation

Buyer-facing edition: 14 October 2026

Control your video viewing, keep and recover your own timecoded research notes, and turn reviewed observations into source-linked project investigations.

A local-first video and research-workspace prototype with controlled exports.

- Adjust viewing controls
- Keep video bookmarks and notes
- Recover private research drafts
- Prepare source-aware project research
