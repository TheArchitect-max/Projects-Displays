# Machine Cognition Research Framework

TA-IP-020 | Reference name: Noa

Assessment date: 2026-11-10

## Product overview

A bounded cognition research framework for repeatable memory and adaptation experiments.

Identified edition: 0.8.0

The 0.8.0 prototype provides a trained predictive-state layer alongside separate count-based adaptation. Training and held-out evaluation are separated, and retained checkpoints bind the recorded research runs. General intelligence, autonomous action and human-equivalent cognition are not established.
