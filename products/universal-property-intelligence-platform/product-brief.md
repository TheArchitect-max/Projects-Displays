# Universal Property Intelligence Platform

TA-IP-106 | Reference name: Vesta | Specialist applications

Structure property and asset evidence, distinguish facts from assumptions, and prepare replacement-cost summaries.

## Product overview

A local property-evidence core with hierarchical asset records, structured CSV/JSON ingestion, explicit observation types, replacement-cost ranges and deterministic reconciliation exports.

## Potential acquirers

- Property documentation software teams
- Insurance evidence specialists
- Facilities and estate inventory teams

## Documented functions

- Organise property, floor, room and asset hierarchies
- Import structured evidence with unit and currency normalization
- Distinguish observations, declarations and inferences
- Review duplicate candidates and deterministic identities
- Export evidence graphs and replacement-cost ranges

## Available output types

- Property and contents registers
- Replacement-cost summaries
- Evidence-linked reports and exports
