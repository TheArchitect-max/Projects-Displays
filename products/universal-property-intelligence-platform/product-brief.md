TA-IP-107 | Reference alias: Vesta | Specialist Vertical Software

## Product overview

A standard-library property evidence core with hierarchy, observed/declared/inferred records, deterministic low/base/high replacement estimates, condition/risk fields, graph and JSON/CSV exports.

- Keep observed, declared and inferred statements distinct
- Calculate supplied-rate replacement-cost ranges
- Export asset graphs, summaries and JSON/CSV packages
