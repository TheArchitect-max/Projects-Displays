# Universal Property Intelligence Platform

TA-IP-107 | Reference name: Vesta | Specialist applications

Structure property and asset evidence, distinguish facts from assumptions, and prepare replacement-cost summaries.

## Product overview

A property-evidence software foundation for organising buildings, rooms, contents and supporting records.

## Potential acquirers

- Property documentation software teams
- Insurance evidence specialists
- Facilities and estate inventory teams

## Documented functions

- Organise property, floor, room and asset records
- Associate supplied evidence with recorded observations
- Distinguish observed facts, declarations and inferences
- Prepare replacement-cost ranges and export summaries

## Available output types

- Property and contents registers
- Replacement-cost summaries
- Evidence-linked reports and exports
