# Narrative Collectibles Platform

TA-IP-108 | Reference name: Scheherazade

Assessment date: 2026-11-10

## Product overview

An object-independent local reference platform for collectible registries, editorial narratives and controlled simulated commerce.

Identified edition: 0.8.0 / R7

A local collectible-registry and narrative studio combining editorial review, simulated commerce, local access controls and portable verification and recovery.

## Potential acquirers

- Collectible registry software teams
- Narrative product studios
- Independent collection operators

## Documented functions

- Manage collectible and edition records
- Review narratives and editorial decisions
- Study simulated order and stock workflows
- Apply local access and privacy controls
- Export offline verification and recovery packages

## Output types

- Collectible registry records
- Editorial review summaries
- Simulated commerce reports
- Verification and recovery packages

## Material scope boundaries

- Commerce uses simulated transactions; real payments, production identity services and hosted operations are excluded.
- Narrative checks do not establish literary originality, translation quality or third-party rights clearance.
- Portable integrity verification does not independently establish ownership, historical truth or external trust.
