# Manuscript Integrity & Publishing Platform

TA-IP-105 | Reference name: Euterpe

Assessment date: 2026-11-10

## Product overview

A manuscript-production prototype for editorial evidence and author-controlled release.

Identified edition: 0.1.0

The offered v0.1.0 editorial QA application supports manuscript preparation, recorded evidence, release gates and an author-controlled publishing handoff. Separate offline staging through v0.7 documents genre/style controls, creative modules and originality workflows. Full staged application source import remains pending and is outside the v0.1.0 asking and recreation scope.

## Conditions before handover

- Freeze the agreed v0.1 source and delivery manifest, including recipient acceptance of the supported Python environment and manual book handoff.
- Agree the permission and transfer treatment of the embedded author-supplied QA protocol and Word template; author manuscripts, creative archives and pseudonym rights are separately scoped.
- Complete the delivery-specific component/provenance and security declaration; no independent legal title or security certification is established.
- If the v0.5 offline build is requested, first import and verify its complete source archive and repeat its source-specific tests; a staging document does not include that application code.
- Perform real editorial, factual, accessibility, visual DOCX and distributor checks for every actual book before author authorization.
