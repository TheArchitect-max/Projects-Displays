### Editorial workbench

A local editorial production and publication-control workbench for authors, editors and small publishing teams. Each book keeps its own manuscript, evidence, defects and explicit owner decision.

- Chapter writing and reproducible DOCX assembly; import ordinary DOCX, Markdown or plain text.
- Explicit author release authorization and an offline DOCX/metadata handoff for manual Draft2Digital upload.
- Local read-only dashboard and CLI; no externally hosted account or billing system.

### Separately documented style studio

The staged Literary Style Studio records 57 solo or multi-select literary modes in seven groups, eight adjustable 1–5 controls, 27 genre profiles and book/chapter/scene scope. Chiaroscuro, Tenebrism and Sfumato are configurable signature principles. The complete author archive is retained; creative eligibility is separate from archival preservation.

- 57 selectable literary modes in seven groups; eight adjustable controls; 27 genre profiles.
- Chiaroscuro, Tenebrism and Sfumato are configurable creative principles.
- The style-studio documentation is separate from a fully imported and independently executed application release.

### Editorial acceptance

Human editorial approval governs each book and its release.

- Recorded QA evidence and human approval do not independently certify literary merit, originality, factual truth, rights clearance or accessibility.
- Ordinary DOCX paragraph inspection and simple formatting need full rendering checks for each real book.
- Manual Draft2Digital handoff; no distributor login, upload, sales or publication automation.
- The documented style presets prepare creative direction; no AI author model training or autonomous manuscript rewriting is established.
