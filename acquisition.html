BUYER GUIDE

Acquisition, with a defined perimeter.

Acquire the existing work AS IS. Agree the exact rights, delivery and acceptance for your intended use.

Choose an individually scoped asset

Each product page identifies one commercial asset with its own reference, asking price, present scope and handover conditions. Use the collection filters to identify relevant work. Related products can be discussed together while retaining separate rights and delivery perimeters.

Prepare a concrete enquiry

Select an asset or several assets and prepare an enquiry draft. State your intended use, target market, requested delivery and any acceptance needs. The exhibition creates a local draft; send it through the seller channel that provided this package.

Agree a controlled review

Agree the evaluation purpose and confidentiality perimeter before any separate non-public disclosure. Product-level presentations do not include implementation detail. The seller and buyer define which evidence and delivery material are needed for the intended transaction.

Define the purchase and acceptance

AS IS acquisition of the seller-owned first-party software asset and specifically agreed transferable work products. The final written agreement defines the rights, exact delivery, permitted use and acceptance conditions.

The delivery schedule identifies the exact accepted build, included documentation, transferable rights, applicable third-party obligations and acceptance criteria. Any additional development, integration or support is separately agreed.

Understand the offered perimeter

Candidate handover

  • Present first-party software within the agreed asset perimeter
  • Associated original product documentation and instructions
  • Existing tests, evaluation records and handover material, where retained
  • The specifically agreed transferable first-party rights

Separate or excluded

  • Third-party software, data, models, media and service rights beyond their own terms
  • User, author, personal and customer content; private accounts and credentials
  • Future development, hosting, hardware, operational support and business income unless expressly agreed
  • Internal development methods, prompts, design reasoning and other non-public know-how unless expressly agreed

Close under written terms

An enquiry, a displayed asking reference and access to this package do not themselves transfer intellectual property. Final consideration, taxes, payment, delivery and rights pass under the definitive agreement. This exhibition provides no checkout or payment processing.

Seller and rights position

The seller identifies this collection under TheArchitect-max / The Architect. The first-party rights position is seller-declared ownership or control to the extent transferable. The legal contracting identity and title-supporting records are agreed for the transaction. Third-party rights stay governed by their own terms; independent legal title or freedom-to-operate opinions are not represented here.
